Control PowerPoint with your voice

HeritageAI makes hands-free PowerPoint presentation control possible with natural voice commands. Instead of repeatedly returning to your computer during a presentation, you can use HeritageAI to move to the next slide, return to a previous slide or jump to a specific slide.

Designed for students, lecturers, trainers, speakers and professionals, HeritageAI combines voice recognition with PowerPoint automation to create a more natural presentation experience.
