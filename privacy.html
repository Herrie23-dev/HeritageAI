HeritageAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

HeritageAI ("HeritageAI", "we", "our", or "the application") is a voice-controlled PowerPoint presentation assistant for Windows. This Privacy Policy explains how HeritageAI handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

HeritageAI is designed to operate locally on your computer. The application does not intentionally collect, sell, or transmit personal information to us.

Voice input used to control presentations is processed locally by the application. HeritageAI does not intentionally upload your voice recordings to a remote server for processing.

2. Microphone Access

HeritageAI requires access to your computer's microphone so that it can recognize voice commands such as starting a presentation, navigating between slides, and stopping the presentation assistant.

Microphone access is used only for the application's voice-control functionality.

3. PowerPoint Access

HeritageAI interacts with Microsoft PowerPoint on your computer to provide presentation controls such as next slide, previous slide, and direct slide navigation.

HeritageAI does not intentionally upload your PowerPoint presentations or presentation content to our servers.

4. Local Processing

Voice recognition functionality is designed to run locally on the user's computer using locally available software and speech-recognition resources.

5. Third-Party Services

HeritageAI may be distributed through third-party platforms such as GitHub. Those platforms may collect information according to their own privacy policies when you visit their websites or download the application.

HeritageAI does not control the privacy practices of third-party services.

6. Children's Privacy

HeritageAI is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not intentionally collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when HeritageAI's features or data practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or HeritageAI's privacy practices, you can contact the HeritageAI project through its official GitHub repository.

HeritageAI on GitHub

9. Summary

HeritageAI is designed with local processing in mind. The application does not intentionally collect or transmit users' personal information, voice recordings, or PowerPoint presentation content to HeritageAI servers.
